# Full-Stack Assessment: Product Discovery & Purchase Confidence

## Context

LAAM is a marketplace for South Asian fashion. Customers often browse many similar-looking products across different brands, sizes, prices, delivery timelines, and stock availability.

A common user problem is:

> “I like a product, but I am not fully confident whether it is available in my size, whether it can arrive on time, whether the price is final, or whether there are better alternatives.”

This lack of confidence can lead to drop-offs before add-to-cart or checkout.

## Product Problem

Design and build a small full-stack experience that helps a customer make a confident purchase decision for a fashion product.

The solution should improve the customer’s ability to answer questions like:
- Is this product available for me?
- Is my preferred size in stock?
- What will I actually pay?
- Can I trust the delivery promise?
- Are there similar alternatives if this product is not suitable?

You do not need to build a complete marketplace or checkout system. Focus on a small but meaningful slice of the experience.

## Your Task

Define the scope, technical approach, and implementation yourself.

You should decide:
- What user flow to build
- What data model is needed
- What backend APIs are needed
- What frontend experience is needed
- What tradeoffs to make given the time limit
- What edge cases are important
- What you would defer and why

You may use mock data, in-memory storage, JSON files, SQLite, or any lightweight setup.
